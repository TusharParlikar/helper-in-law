JUDGMENT

1. The appellant was convicted under Section 302 IPC for causing the death of his neighbour during a quarrel over a boundary wall on 17 June 2010 and sentenced to imprisonment for life.

2. Learned counsel for the appellant submitted that the incident occurred in a sudden fight in the heat of passion and that there was no premeditation.

3. The question is whether the case falls within Exception 4 to Section 300 IPC, which would reduce the offence from murder to culpable homicide not amounting to murder.

Page 1 of 2
4. Exception 4 to Section 300 IPC applies where the death is caused without premeditation in a sudden fight in the heat of passion upon a sudden quarrel, and the offender has not taken undue advantage or acted in a cruel or unusual manner.

5. The evidence shows that the appellant picked up a wooden stick lying at the spot and struck a single blow. He did not carry any weapon to the scene.

6. A single blow with a weapon picked up at the spot during a sudden quarrel is a strong indication that there was no premeditation.

Page 2 of 2
7. We hold that the offence falls under Section 304 Part I IPC and not under Section 302 IPC. The conviction is altered accordingly and the sentence is reduced to rigorous imprisonment for ten years.
