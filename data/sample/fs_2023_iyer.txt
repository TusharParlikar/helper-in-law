JUDGMENT

1. The petitioner wife challenges an order of the Family Court rejecting her application for maintenance under Section 125 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

2. The Family Court held that the petitioner was a qualified teacher capable of earning and was therefore not entitled to maintenance.

3. The question is whether a wife who is qualified but not actually earning is disentitled to maintenance under Section 125 CrPC.

4. The mere capacity of the wife to earn is not a ground to deny maintenance under Section 125 CrPC. What is relevant is whether she is actually able to maintain herself.

5. Maintenance under Section 125 CrPC is a measure of social justice intended to prevent destitution and vagrancy.

6. The respondent husband earns Rs. 1,20,000 per month. The petitioner is awarded maintenance of Rs. 25,000 per month from the date of the application.

7. The revision petition is allowed.
