JUDGMENT

1. This reference arises from conflicting views on the admissibility of electronic records without a certificate under Section 65B(4) of the Indian Evidence Act.

2. Learned counsel for the appellant submitted that the decision in Rohit Verma v. State of NCT of Delhi, (2016) 2 FSR 404 requires reconsideration.

3. The issue is whether a certificate under Section 65B(4) is a condition precedent to the admissibility of secondary evidence of an electronic record.

4. Section 65B is a complete code on the admissibility of electronic records. A certificate under Section 65B(4) is mandatory where secondary evidence of an electronic record is produced.

5. A party who is not in possession of the device may apply to the court to direct the person in charge of the device to produce the certificate.

6. The view taken in Rohit Verma v. State of NCT of Delhi, (2016) 2 FSR 404 that the certificate may be dispensed with is not correct in law and is accordingly overruled.

7. The certificate is not required where the original device itself is produced before the court as primary evidence.

8. The reference is answered accordingly.
