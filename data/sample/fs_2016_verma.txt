CRIMINAL APPEAL NO. 404 OF 2016

Neutral Citation: 2016 FSC 404

JUDGMENT

1. The prosecution relied on call detail records produced as computer printouts without a certificate under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act.

2. The question is whether a certificate under Section 65B(4) of the Evidence Act is mandatory for the admissibility of electronic records.

3. Where the party producing the electronic record is not in possession of the device from which it was produced, it cannot be expected to furnish a certificate under Section 65B(4).

4. We hold that the requirement of a certificate under Section 65B(4) is procedural and may be relaxed in the interest of justice where the party is not in possession of the device.

5. The appeal is dismissed.
