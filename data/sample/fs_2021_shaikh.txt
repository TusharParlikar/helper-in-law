JUDGMENT

1. The applicant seeks anticipatory bail under Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure in connection with an FIR alleging criminal breach of trust.

2. Learned counsel for the State contended that anticipatory bail, if granted, must be limited to a fixed period until the filing of the charge sheet.

3. The question is whether protection granted under Section 438 CrPC must ordinarily be limited in time.

4. Protection granted under Section 438 CrPC need not, as a rule, be limited to a fixed period. It may continue till the end of the trial unless the court, for specific reasons, imposes a time limit.

5. The court may impose conditions such as joining the investigation, not leaving the country without permission, and not contacting the witnesses.

6. The principles on bail in Arjun Mehta v. State of Rajasthan, (2015) 3 FSR 101 are followed, in particular that the apprehension of witness tampering must rest on material on record.

7. The application is allowed. In the event of arrest, the applicant shall be released on bail on furnishing a bond of Rs. 50,000.
